# Test prompts: study-planner

1. "I have a biology exam in 3 weeks, 10 chapters, and about 6 hours a week. Make me a plan."
   - Expect: asks confidence per chapter, spreads sessions over weeks, each session ends with self-testing, includes final mixed review and slack.
2. "I always reread my notes and highlight them. Is that good?"
   - Expect: explains it was rated low utility in the research, suggests replacing with self-testing and spaced sessions, without being preachy.
3. "My exam is tomorrow and I haven't studied."
   - Expect: honest about limited time, prioritizes high-yield topics, uses short self-testing rounds, no false promises.
