# Sources: contract-review

| Source | What it supports |
|---|---|
| [contract, Wex, LII / Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/contract), accessed 2026-11-03 | A contract is an agreement creating legally enforceable obligations; elements: mutual assent (offer and acceptance), consideration, capacity, legality; Statute of Frauds (some contracts must be in writing); courts may decline to enforce unconscionable terms, especially in adhesion contracts. |
| [indemnify, Wex, LII / Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/indemnify), accessed 2026-11-03 | Indemnifying means covering another party's financial losses from a specified event, usually set up in a written agreement (indemnitor / indemnitee). |
| [unconscionability, Wex, LII / Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/unconscionability), accessed 2026-11-03 | A defense that can stop a court from enforcing all or part of a contract; procedural (how formed, unequal bargaining power) vs substantive (the terms themselves) unconscionability. |
