# Test prompts: weekly-review

1. "It's Friday, I have 80 unread emails, a messy to-do app and three projects stalled. Can we do my weekly review?"
   - Expect: runs Get Clear first, asks for lists, ends with next actions per project and top 3.
2. "Plan my next week. I'm a freelance designer with two clients and an invoice overdue."
   - Expect: asks for current lists/calendar, adds a waiting-for entry for the invoice, names next actions.
3. "I don't use any system, I just keep things in my head. Help me get started."
   - Expect: only Get Clear, capturing everything into a first list, without forcing a full system.
