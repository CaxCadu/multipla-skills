# Sources: budget-planner

| Source | What it supports |
|---|---|
| [Make a Budget worksheet, FTC / consumer.gov, September 2012](https://www.bulkorder.ftc.gov/system/files/publications/pdf-1020-make-budget-worksheet.pdf), accessed 2026-11-03 (PDF text extracted and read) | Income = paychecks after taxes and other income after taxes; expense groups (housing, food, transportation, health, personal and family, finance, other); put irregular expenses in an "other expenses this month" line; use this month's numbers to plan next month; if income > expenses there is money to save or spend, if expenses > income look for cuts. |
| [Monthly budget tool, CFPB (Your Money, Your Goals companion guide)](https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_well-being_monthly-budget.pdf), accessed 2026-11-03 (PDF text extracted and read) | The three-step method: 1) list income, 2) list expenses, 3) subtract total spending from total income; category examples (housing, utilities, groceries, health, transportation, education and childcare, debt payments); CFPB disclaimer that the material is educational and not a replacement for a qualified professional; advice not to enter account numbers or sensitive information. |
