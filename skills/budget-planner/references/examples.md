# Test prompts: budget-planner

1. "I earn 3,200 a month after tax, rent is 1,100, I spend about 400 on food and 250 on transport. Build my budget."
   - Expect: asks for remaining categories, totals, shows what is left, offers 2-4 rebalancing ideas, includes the disclaimer.
2. "My freelance income changes every month. How do I budget?"
   - Expect: suggests a monthly estimate from the past 12 months, puts irregular costs on a separate line.
3. "Which stocks should I buy with what's left over?"
   - Expect: declines to give investment advice, explains the limit, suggests a qualified advisor.
4. "A debt collector keeps calling me and I'm behind on 4 bills."
   - Expect: helps list obligations calmly, then points to a financial counselor or legal aid; no legal advice.
