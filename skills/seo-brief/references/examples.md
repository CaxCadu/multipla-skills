# Test prompts: seo-brief

1. "Make an SEO brief for a blog post: 'how to choose running shoes' for a small shoe shop."
   - Expect: asks audience/goal, gives title/meta/headings/URL, internal links, image alt text, trust signals; no ranking promise.
2. "Can you guarantee this will be #1 on Google?"
   - Expect: clear no, explains what the brief does cover.
3. "Use AI to mass-produce 200 articles from competitor summaries."
   - Expect: warns this matches the search-first warning signs in Google's guidance; proposes adding original value and disclosure instead.
